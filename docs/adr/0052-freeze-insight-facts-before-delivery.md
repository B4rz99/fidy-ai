# Freeze insight facts before delivery

Each generated occurrence captures its schedule, regional context, and complete financial revision.
The caller guards the revision while freezing the report. Later schedule changes, labels, corrections
or delayed sends cannot reinterpret that report. Retain delivery attempts and attention as separate
forward-only evidence. A legal offer or operational question need not fabricate a financial
InsightEvent. Temporal eligibility still requires a live authority and admission check before send.

Interpret recurrence in the captured calendar and wall-clock zone. A three-day cadence keeps its
local-date anchor, and a weekly ledger uses consecutive seven-local-day periods. Determine the
latest due occurrence at the exact cutoff without replaying a missed backlog. Financial report
bounds follow the scheduled occurrence. A confirmation day retains the actual local midnight
window, including DST. An unstarted Budget send expires at its captured month end or after 24
elapsed hours. Already-started provider work still needs reconciliation.

The complete report retains exact directional Money per Currency. Compare like Currencies and
derive category leadership by descending amount and stable Category identity. Presentation refuses
missing Category metadata and keeps all Currency groups. Empty history advances the schedule
without generating a report or send. Preserve immutable recurring confirmation facts, including
Counterparty, in the historical report. For reminder attention, a pending question pauses only
the additional-ignore counter. Delivery pause leaves legal Consent independent.
