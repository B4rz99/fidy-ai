## RNBD applicability is independent of the remaining privacy duties

[Decreto 090 of 2018-01-18](https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/sites/default/files/Normograma/docs/decreto_0090_2018.htm),
article 1, amends Decreto 1074 article **2.2.2.26.1.2**: registration covers controllers that are
companies/nonprofits with total assets **above 100,000 UVT**, and public legal entities. A Colombian
S.A.S. is not exempt merely because it is developing a product or has no bank connection. The
decree expressly preserves other Ley 1581 duties for those outside registration. Article 2 retains
registration within two months of creation for databases created after the historical transition
periods; those 2018–2019 dates are not new-company grace periods.

SIC [concept 22-300986, dated 2022-09-12](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/conceptos/22-300986%20.pdf),
section 3.2, measures total assets at **December 31 of the preceding year**. Its
[2022-11-16 publication summary](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/publicaciones/boletin-juridico/concepto/cuales-personas-estan-obligadas-realizar-el-registro-de-bases-de-datos-personales-en-el-rnbd)
confirms that unregistered controllers still need policies, applicable authorization, rights procedures
and security. The owner has since supplied accountant-verified assets and their accounting date; version 2 of the
restricted applicability record preserves them without publishing financial particulars. Fidy's
incorporation date and the appropriate preceding-year/opening-balance measurement basis remain
unresolved. Do not substitute a current snapshot for an unestablished historical measurement. A qualified reviewer must resolve the appropriate first-year
measurement and the precise time at which registration becomes due. Total assets are not subscribed
capital, income, net equity or a guess based on company size. Obtain a balance sheet/certified asset
figure with its accounting date and legal entity, and verify private company legal nature from the
corporate record.

[DIAN Resolution 000238 of 2025-12-15, article 1](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/resolucion_dian_0238_2025.htm)
sets **2026 UVT = COP 52,374**. Thus 100,000 units at that value equal **COP 5,237,400,000**
(calculated multiplication). This is a friendly 2026 benchmark, not a determination of which UVT
year to pair with Fidy's particular measurement period; the reviewer must record that basis.

**Boundary inconsistency:** current Title V §2.1, printed page 18, agrees with the decree's strictly
higher threshold. Section 2.2, page 20, instead contains the incomplete wording
“activos totales o superiores”. This does not justify silently coding `>= 100000` or declaring exact
equality exempt. Record equality as requiring qualified resolution against the decree and authoritative
clarification. No current asset decision is made here.

## Privacy function and corporate responsibility

Decreto 1074 article **2.2.2.25.4.4** requires each controller/processor to designate a person or area
for privacy and Titular requests. SIC reproduces that provision and explains the role in
[concept 21-17773 of 2021-02-25, page 7](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/conceptos/21-17773.pdf).
It permits a designated person; it does not itself establish an additional employee or mandatory backup.
User authorization establishes Orlando as Fidy's sole operator; a dated internal designation should
record decision authority, contact, responsibilities and the absence of backup honestly.

[SIC Circular Externa 003, issued 2024-08-22 and published 2024-08-23](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/transparencia/normativa/circular-externa-3-de-2024-de-la-superintendencia-de-industria-y-comercio-instrucciones-para-los-administradores-societarios)
is separately relevant to administrators. The [official municipal legal reproduction](https://www.alcaldiabogota.gov.co/sisjur/normas/Norma1.jsp?dt=S&i=163877)
records Diario Oficial **52.857**, instructions I–V on effective governance/security and
co-responsibility where administrators jointly determine particular processing purposes or means.
Appointment of a privacy operator does not transfer away the company's or administrators' duties.

## Incident trigger, clock and recipient

[Ley 1581 of 2012](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=49981),
articles **17(n)** and **18(k)**, requires controllers and processors to inform the authority when
security-code violations and risks in administering Titular information arise. Article **4(g)** and
articles **17(d)/18(b)** require protective measures. An unavailable deployment alone is not proof of
a personal-data incident; record the assessment and evidence. Conversely, do not wait for proof of
criminal exfiltration when personal information was lost, altered, stolen or accessed without authority.

Current Title V **§2.1(f)(ii), printed page 19**, supplies the operational deadline: **15 working days
following detection and communication to the person/area handling the incident**. It covers registered
controllers; nonregistered controllers and processors also report through the dedicated application
or other official receiving channels. This is independent of the asset threshold. Preserve detection
and owner-awareness timestamps separately. Fidy's single operator normally makes them coincide;
an approval meeting, containment, final forensic certainty or provider answer does not restart them.
The earlier detection time should also be tracked as a conservative internal target.

[Law 4 of 1913, article 62](https://www.igac.gov.co/sites/default/files/transparencia/normograma/ley_4_de_1913_0.pdf)
excludes holidays/nonworking days from official day terms unless stated otherwise. A deadline record
needs the Colombia calendar, actual trigger, excluded dates and reviewer confirmation of SIC working
days/closures; `15 * 24 hours` is wrong. An internal earlier target is not a statutory extension.
The reviewed sources do not settle Saturday treatment for every filing scenario. Document whether
Saturdays count rather than silently treating all working days as Monday–Friday. For fictional
detection/awareness on **2026-10-16**, a conservative six-day calendar excluding Sundays and the
[November 2 holiday](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=4954) reaches day 15 on **November 4**; a Monday–Friday calendar excluding that holiday
reaches **November 9**. Those are exercise assumptions requiring qualified calendar confirmation,
not competing certified statutory deadlines. No GDPR 72-hour rule is inferred from these provisions.

[official PQRSF entry](https://servicioslinea.sic.gov.co/servilinea/pqrsf/); the login footer displays
**contactenos@sic.gov.co**. These are receiving channels, not proof an incident was filed.

The 2025 manual §6.2, pages **90–102**, documents **INCIDENTES → Incidentes de Seguridad → Registrar
Nuevo Incidente de Seguridad** for obliged and nonobliged subjects. Nonobliged users continue via the
incident prompt; new users first complete controller information. Section 6.2.1 uses **Otra base de
Datos** for unregistered databases, then data categories. Its form screenshot, page **94**, shows type,
incident date, knowledge date, cause, description, affected Titular count and a supporting attachment
control. Counts must fit the affected database population. These are documented fields, not a
verified authenticated form or an exhaustive current validation contract.

The same page explicitly says a filed incident cannot be modified; **Guardar y Radicar** is the
submission boundary. Do not promise editable incident reports or assume a draft equals filing.

For incomplete initial facts, preserve unknowns, submit truthful known facts in time, and retain the
receipt/radicado. For later findings/corrections/closure, prepare a dated supplement referencing that
radicado through the official receiving channel; confirm the authority's accepted follow-up route.
This is a proposed operating approach, not a verified module amendment/closure function. A failed
form attempt needs evidence and escalation through the current official alternative, not a reset clock.

## Response guidance, Titulares, providers and bank duties

The official [SIC incident guide, 2020](https://www.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/files/Publicaciones/Guia_gestion_incidentes_dic21_2020.pdf),
announced [2020-12-21](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/noticias/superindustria-expide-guia-para-la-gestion-de-incidentes-de-seguridad-en-el-tratamiento-de-datos-personales),
is guidance, not a legal certification. Pages **10–11** recommend prompt processor notification,
contractual contacts, response records and reporting proof; outsourcing does not remove processors'
own notification obligation. Pages **16–20** treat containment, impact assessment, reporting,
communication and prevention as concurrent work. Page **19** recommends telling affected Titulares
when communication can avert/mitigate harm, using understandable practical advice. Record that decision
separately from SIC reportability; no general GDPR-style risk exemption or automatic 72-hour Titular
notification deadline is established here.

Review Ley 1266 applicability separately for actual financial-information roles; Chapter I of Title V
is not automatically imposed simply because a Transaction contains Money. Likewise, no evidence here
establishes Fidy as an SFC-supervised institution or imports supervised entities' reporting deadlines.
[Bancolombia's public developer support terms](https://soportedeveloper-portal.bancolombia.com/hc/es-419/p/terminos_de_uso)
mention support forms/chat for incidents, but do not establish Fidy's contracted security incident
channel or SLA. Those remain account/contract-specific and unverified; do not send incidents to a
guessed address or use the public developer community for confidential details.
