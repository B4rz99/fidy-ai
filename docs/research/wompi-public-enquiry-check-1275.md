# Wompi public documentation check for #1275

Checked: **2026-10-16**. Scope: official Colombia merchant rules, current privacy documents,
registration/change guidance, support channels and payment-source/acceptance documentation relevant
to the proposed enquiry. This is a targeted public-source check, not a claim to have inspected every
Wompi document, qualified legal approval, account acceptance evidence or a provider rehearsal.
No provider was contacted, account changed or data deleted by this research.

## Public answers that should be removed from the enquiry

The [merchant rules page](https://www.wompi.co/es/co/reglamento-comercios/) labels its document
September 2026. Its [57-page PDF](https://www.wompi.co/assets/downloadble/reglamento-Comercios-Colombia.pdf)
is **V4-2026**. Sections 2.2.1 and 2.2.18 distinguish personal/company authority and electronic
acceptance evidence; section 17.2 sets a publication/change mechanism, including deemed acceptance
after ten calendar days for active merchants that do not request cancellation. Section 16 permits
Wompi to act as controller for authorized payment data, including API/tokenization flows. Section 12
requires merchants to retain transaction-support documents for twelve months; that is not Wompi's
own universal retention period. These public clauses do not identify this merchant's accepted
versions, acceptance events, legal entity or enabled service agreements.

The [current privacy page](https://www.wompi.co/es/co/politica-de-privacidad) is labelled October 2026.
Its linked [20-page policy](https://www.wompi.co/assets/downloadble/politica-de-privacidad-wompi.pdf),
with printed effective date **2026-08-26** (p.20), supersedes the older PDF as this review's current
publication source. It describes Wompi as controller; suppression may use deletion, blocking or
anonymization, subject to legal/contractual retention. General periods: clients ten years after the
relationship ends; payers ten years after the last transaction or relationship ends (pp.13–14).
Complaints require claimant identification, facts, standing and response contact; consultation and
complaint deadlines and extensions are published (pp.16–17). The policy provides
`notificacionesjudiciales@wompi.co` and the website's data-treatment/revocation request category
(pp.14,17), and allows national/international transfers/transmissions with safeguards (p.18).
It does not establish this account's actual recipients/countries or backup-expiry evidence.

Public [registration guidance](https://wompi.com/es/co/ayuda/como-crear-cuenta) distinguishes
registration using a person's identification from registration using the company's NIT; company
registration requires the representative's identity document and RUT. It includes acceptance of
terms and data authorization. This establishes an available company-registration path, not a
supported migration of an existing personal merchant.

The [immutable-fields article](https://soporte.wompi.co/hc/es-419/articles/360054853494--Cu%C3%A1l-es-la-informaci%C3%B3n-que-no-se-puede-modificar-en-la-plataforma),
updated 2025-02-06, says the registered identification number and email cannot be changed. The
[approved-merchant changes article](https://soporte.wompi.co/hc/es-419/articles/360046142534--Qu%C3%A9-informaci%C3%B3n-puedo-modificar-una-vez-haya-sido-aprobado-vinculado-mi-comercio-en-la-pasarela-de-pagos),
updated 2024-06-27, allows requests to change contact details, trade name, representative name and
settlement account after validation. Changing a trade name or representative is not evidence that
the contractual person/NIT can change. The [merchant-update article](https://soporte.wompi.co/hc/es-419/articles/360020960633--Qu%C3%A9-debo-hacer-para-actualizar-la-informaci%C3%B3n-como-comercio-en-la-pasarela)
requires supporting RUT/chamber documents for applicable updates and expressly requires new
registration for a registration-email change. It does not expressly prescribe the natural-person
to S.A.S. transition or preservation of existing payment sources.

[Acceptance-token documentation](https://docs.wompi.co/docs/colombia/tokens-de-aceptacion/) explains
the public mechanism for presenting payer documents, explicit acceptance and submitting both tokens
when creating transactions/payment sources. This is separate from retrieving the merchant's own
historical contract acceptance. The [payment-source guide](https://docs.wompi.co/docs/colombia/fuentes-de-pago/)
distinguishes sandbox from production and documents tokenized recurring payments. Its DaviPlata
description mentions cancellation/unsubscription using the source ID, but this review found no
documented guarantee that such cancellation erases transaction, fraud, backup or downstream copies.
Do not label source cancellation as verified privacy erasure or activate a payment method for testing.

## Contact route

The [current general-support article](https://soporte.wompi.co/hc/es-419/articles/360020766194--Cu%C3%A1l-es-el-horario-de-atenci%C3%B3n-y-los-canales-de-comunicaci%C3%B3n-que-ofrece-la-plataforma),
updated 2025-12-11, offers WhatsApp and the support form, with working-day service hours and estimated
response times. It does not establish a current general merchant-support email. Older, still-public
[QR support guidance](https://soporte.wompi.co/hc/es-419/articles/10692769745683--Si-tengo-una-duda-con-las-comisiones-descontadas-para-este-medio-de-pago-con-quien-debo-contactarme)
lists `ayuda@wompi.co`, but its QR-specific 2022 context is insufficient to treat it as a newly
verified general migration route. Use the current privacy-policy address for the limited privacy
enquiry, requesting routing if needed; do not send attachments or identification documents merely
to ask about capability. A form requiring additional personal fields needs the corresponding
approved sharing scope before submission.
