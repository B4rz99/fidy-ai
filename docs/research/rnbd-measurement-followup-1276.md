## What is established

[Decreto 090 of 2018, article 1](https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/sites/default/files/Normograma/docs/decreto_0090_2018.htm)
amends Decreto 1074 §2.2.2.26.1.2: private companies and nonprofits are in scope when total assets
exceed **100,000 UVT**; public legal entities are a separate category. The article does not prescribe
a valuation date or an opening-balance substitute. Its recitals preserve other privacy duties for
entities outside registration. Article 2's two-month rule concerns creation of an in-scope database,
not incorporation of a company or a universal two-month exemption for a new business.

The published [SIC concept 22-300986 of 2022-09-12](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/conceptos/22-300986%20.pdf),
PDF pages **6–7**, adds a prior-year measurement: assets recorded at **December 31 of the immediately
preceding year**. Pages **1–2** characterize the response as advisory and decline to decide a
particular person's situation. The source is an official interpretation, not a new decree. This
matches [CPACA article 28, substituted by Ley 1755 of 2015](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=65334):
consultation concepts ordinarily are not compulsory unless another legal rule provides otherwise.

The official [current Title V consolidation](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/normativa/T%C3%ADtulo%20V.%20Circular%20001%20del%209%20de%20enero%20de%202026.%20Publicada%20en%20el%20Diario%20Oficial%20No.%2053.362%20del%2009%20de%20enero%20de%202026.pdf),
labelled Circular 001 of **2026-01-09**, Chapter II §§**2.1–2.3**, PDF pages **18–21**, was downloaded
and read again. It repeats the asset scope, registration procedure and updates but supplies no
express first-year valuation substitute or exception for a company without a preceding December 31. Its annual January–March update window applies to obliged registered controllers; it does not
by itself settle first-year applicability or defer every company's initial registration.

## Application to the supplied facts

The owner supplied a below-threshold total-assets snapshot, expressly confirmed as accountant
verified, with an accounting date. Those facts are already recorded privately; do not request the
same amount/date again or call them unverified by an accountant. Public research cannot establish
whether the company existed at the preceding year-end or its assets then.

**Inference:** the supplied snapshot supports a below-threshold conclusion on its stated date. It
does not alone establish that the preceding December 31 balance was below threshold: assets can
change. Conversely, if the company did not exist then, treating nonexistent historical accounts as
zero would invent a fact and a first-year rule absent from these sources. A current-year snapshot
is not automatically disqualified by an express statutory first-year prohibition; the unresolved
point is its reasoned use when no prior year-end exists.

For a 2026 decision, the smallest factual fork is therefore **whether Fidy legally existed on
2025-12-31**. An exact incorporation date or a private corporate-document reference would establish
that fork without publishing company documents. This is a narrower request than another general
asset statement or a request to buy a legal certificate.

## No established mandatory lawyer certificate

The decree's RNBD scope/registration articles do not condition a non-registration decision on an
external lawyer's signature. [Decreto 1074 §2.2.2.25.4.4](https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/sites/default/files/Normograma/docs/decreto_1074_2015_pr026.htm)
requires a designated person or area for privacy requests; it does not prescribe a second employee,
external lawyer or backup in that provision. This is limited to the inspected provisions, not a
claim that every sector-specific or contractual requirement has been ruled out.

Current **#1276 separately requires a qualified reviewer**. Public research does not establish an
across-the-board lawyer-hiring obligation, and AI research or a code review must not be relabelled
as that human qualification. The issue criterion remains unsatisfied unless actual qualified review
is supplied or the owner explicitly changes that acceptance requirement; confirmed sole-operator
status alone does not silently change it.

Confidence: **high** for the decree's scope, the concept's prior-year wording and its advisory
status; **limited** for first-year application because the incorporation fact and express first-year
authority are absent. No overall compliance or bank-readiness certification is made.
