## Conclusion supported by the supplied facts

**FIDY S.A.S. is not currently required to register its databases in the RNBD on the supplied
facts.** This is a reasoned application of the registration threshold, not an exemption from data
protection law, a permanent exemption, or an independently qualified professional opinion.

[Decreto 090 of 2018, article 1](https://cancilleria.gov.co/sites/default/files/Normograma/docs/decreto_0090_2018.htm)
limits the private-company registration category to total assets **above 100,000 UVT**. Public legal
entities are a separate category. The decree's recitals expressly preserve other privacy duties for
controllers outside registration. It does not require an external lawyer's signature to rely on the
asset scope. Its text does not prescribe a first-year opening-balance rule.

**Application/inference:** the supplied current-year registration evidence establishes a newly
registered private company, and its supplied accounting evidence is below the threshold at the
relevant current-year snapshot. There is no preceding-year company balance to obtain. These facts
support recording **NotRequiredForReviewedPeriod** for that snapshot. An unidentified first-year
valuation provision is not affirmative evidence that registration is required. Recheck at year-end
and when assets, legal nature or applicable rules change; do not invent historical accounts or
interpret new-company status itself as an exemption.

## What the measurement sources actually establish

[SIC concept 22-300986 of 2022-09-12](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/conceptos/22-300986%20.pdf),
previously inspected in the [measurement follow-up](rnbd-measurement-followup-1276.md), describes
preceding-December-31 assets. That advisory interpretation does not expressly resolve the missing
preceding year for a new company. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403 during this recheck; its prior
inspection is distinguished from the newly retrieved resolution below.

[SIC Resolution 17116 of 2022-03-31](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/boletin/docs/Resolucion%2017116.pdf),
PDF pages **1–2**, reproduces an appellant's argument relying on low assets at constitution and the
previous year-end. **That is the appellant's argument, not SIC's holding.** Section 3 and page **5**
instead uphold registration using the much higher assets at the challenged decision's date; the
conclusion on page **7** repeats that current-date basis. This is a particular administrative
decision, not a new general first-year measurement rule. It supports checking changes during the
year rather than assuming prior-year figures or first-year status shield a company whose assets
have subsequently crossed the threshold. It supplies no reason to treat a genuinely
below-threshold current snapshot as above threshold.

The current Title V URL cited in the earlier research also returned HTTP 403 in this recheck; no
newer circular or explicit first-year substitute was verified here. The conclusion therefore rests
on the retrieved decree, supplied facts and the expressly bounded interpretation above, not a claim
to have freshly inspected every current circular page.
