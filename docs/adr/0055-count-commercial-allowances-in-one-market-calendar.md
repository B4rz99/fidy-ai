# Count commercial allowances in one market calendar

Free allowances count concrete accepted units in independent meters under a half-open
America/Bogota month. One market calendar prevents a User time-zone change from resetting usage.
Trial and Pro carry no visible Free meter, and activity performed with Pro access does not
consume a Free unit. Commercial allowance and security admission enforce distinct policies.

A retry reference identifies a prior admission and carries no authority. Its absolute lifetime
starts at first admission, including across month rollover. The owner consults replay before it
decides on a new unit and rechecks capacity in the guarded acceptance commit.

Wire meter metadata expresses reset as an absolute UTC instant. Security Retry-After delays
remain separate. Inspection stays callable when the commercial canonical-call meter is exhausted.

Hosted saved-Transaction history counts once per User and Turn at the release of new retained
rows. Commit its consumption and evidence atomically. Aggregates, empty or failed reads and
continuity already held by the Turn do not count as new retained rows.
