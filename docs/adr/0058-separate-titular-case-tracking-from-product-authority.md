# Separate Titular case tracking from product authority

The Titular case ledger records manual workflow facts and opaque references to restricted
evidence. An erasure handoff captures the observed request authority and scope so the receiving
owner can make its own decision. A case receipt by itself grants no product authority and
cannot attest deletion. Exact replays preserve the original receipt without executing product work.

Calculate conservative internal response targets from the original intake date, including
Saturdays and excluding Sundays and the maintained Colombian holiday table. Verification
does not reset the clock. The holiday table covers 2026 and 2027, including moved observances
under Ley 51 de 1983. Unsupported years and shorter applicable deadlines need operator review.
The two-month completion review date prompts a decision. It never abandons a case
automatically or extends its response target.
